Goal Contributions Across Europe’s Top Five Football Leagues (2021–2022)

Across 2,920 individual players in the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and Ligue 1 during the 2021–2022 season, total goal contributions (goals plus assists) rise steadily with playing time, but the rate of production depends heavily on position. Elite forwards separate sharply from the rest of the field—led by Kylian Mbappé (46 goal contributions), Karim Benzema (39), Robert Lewandowski (38), and Mohamed Salah (36)—while midfielders form a middle tier and defenders and goalkeepers rarely exceed five contributions even with over 3,000 minutes played.
